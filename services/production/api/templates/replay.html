Blind replay

The market up to a moment, and nothing after it. Take it or skip it.

Some of these moments are when you really entered. The others are random moments from the same months, on the same market, taken the same way. You won't be told which until you've decided.

Each card shows the {{ Figure(window, "minutes")|count }} minutes before the moment. After you decide, you see whether it was yours and what the market did over the next {{ Figure(horizon, "minutes")|count }} minutes, in points, before charges.

Only trades more than {{ Figure(old_after, "days")|count }} days old are used: a recent chart is remembered, and memory would decide for you.

Most one-minute bars are ordinary. The random moments are drawn from every minute, so the cards look the way a chart usually does.
