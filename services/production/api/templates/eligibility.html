What this page is not

A check with enough to work with has not been run, and a check without enough has found nothing — there is nothing yet to find either way. None of this says anything about how you trade. It says what your file can and cannot support.

We do not say here what each check asks. They belong to the research library, and naming them in our own words before they have been run would be telling you what they would say.
