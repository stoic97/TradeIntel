# FB-0078 · decline (to the candidate register, "2026 shift" set)

**The claim (T-001):** his ₹ at risk per trade, and how it changed from 2025 to 2026, on NIFTY. The figures are in the tracker.

**Declined, for three reasons:**
- The size of his risk per trade is a **fact**, not a hypothesis: R1 `size_dispersion_tail` (fact bootstrap) is the core's fact for it. The claim's added content is that it *changed* between years. That is a structural-break question no core test asks, and a new test is a MAJOR version that Phase 0 doesn't build (Annex B).
- The year split was chosen after the look, on the generating sample.
- NIFTY is not yet calibrated (`scope.transfer_rule`).

**What still reads the year shift without a new test:** every core test's mandatory **P1** temporal-halves check reports whether an effect holds in both halves of a history. That is where a 2025-vs-2026 change shows up honestly (see FB-0097).
