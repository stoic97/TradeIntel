Your data

Before you upload

A tradebook is among the most sensitive files you own. Here is exactly what happens to it. The same promises are made in plainer words on plusev.in; if the two ever disagree, this page is the one you agreed to, and we want to hear about it.

What we store

The file you upload, exactly as your broker produced it. The reconstruction we build from it — your trades matched into round trips. And the Journals written from those trades. We also record which lines you open and how long you read them, so that a later version can put the lines that matter to you first; that is a record of what you read, never of anything you type. Nothing else about you.

For how long

Until you delete it.

Who can access it

The two people who run the product: the founder, and the engineer who runs the server. No third party receives the file, and no external service touches anything derived from it — there is no analytics, no error reporting and no email in this product. If that ever changes, this page names the service before it does.

What deletion covers

The file, every artefact derived from it, and every Journal. Backups roll off within 30 days, so your data is gone from those too. What we keep is the record that you consented and the record that you deleted — the proof that both happened.

What deletion cannot undo

Your file and everything derived from it can be deleted at any time. What cannot be undone is that looking at your data informed decisions we have already made about the engine. This is the same as any research partnership, and we say so plainly.

What you are agreeing to

You can withdraw any of these at any time, from your settings.
