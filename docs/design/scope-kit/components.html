Lockup and chips

The PlusEV mark (the expectation operator holding a trade: brackets are the trade, the three dots its fills, unevenly spaced on purpose) at the height of the wordmark, never larger. PlusEV in Plex Sans 600, Scope beneath in Plex Mono 11. Ink on white, white on navy. The mark takes currentColor only: never redraw, respace or recolour it. .lockup, .chip, .chip--on-navy.

Every chart

Inline SVG from scope_geometry.charts.* — data in, { parts: [{ role, d }] } out. Roles map to .chart__ink | __light | __faint | __red | __green | __hatch | __axis | __stroke | __dash. Full size 350 × 96; mini 36 × 14 (white on navy inside a slice).

Worst days — net (gross) per trading day, five worst in ink · charts.worst(daily_net, w, h)

Share — the five days within the whole period, with a bracket · charts.share(share, w, h)

Money — best five (green) and worst five (red) trades · charts.money(best5, worst5, w, h)

Hold — minutes open, winners above, losers below, median ticks · charts.hold(win_minutes, loss_minutes, max_minutes, w, h)

Strike — winners / losers / level as one bar · charts.strike(winners, losers, level, w, h)

Run — one square per trade, the longest losing run in ink · charts.run(n_trades, run_start, run_length, w, h)

Clock — net (gross) by the hour opened; few-trade hours hatched · charts.clock(hour_net, few_hours, w, h)

Down — after two straight losses against every other qualifying trade · charts.down(after_two, others, w, h)

Busy — trades per day, busiest in ink, normal day dashed · charts.busy(day_counts, normal, w, h)

Read — rows read and rows set aside · charts.read(rows_read, rows_total, w, h)

Risk — trades by what they were measured against · charts.risk(budget, stop, premium, nothing, w, h)

Not told — no data; four quiet lines · charts.notold(w, h)

The Scope dial

Inline SVG from scope_geometry.render_dial_svg(build_dial(view, size)). Bezel: ticks every 2.5° in three weights; inner ring; dividers that stop short; upright names at each arc midpoint on a 12 px leader; slice number above the figure; stacked figures where the wedge is too narrow; centre disc with a fine ring. Pressed: the wedge lifts one tone (.is-pressed). Opened: a dot near the rim. Focus: a white ring inside the wedge.
