# Deferrals — the stage-0 ticket register

Developer Manual section 8.7: *a deferral is a ticket number or it didn't happen.* Stage 0
has no tracker, so this file is the register. **A deferral not listed here does not exist.**

| TD-017 | Block-bootstrap interval authority | Section 6.2 point 2: the stationary block bootstrap interval is computed alongside the model interval and **the model interval is widened to match if it is narrower** - "we never report the narrower of two honest answers". M1 currently reports only its own interval | Implementing P7 and the comparison in the fit path | Before the first promoted Finding; also feeds the dependence audit ratio floor of 0.85 |
| TD-018 | `tau_day`'s prior | Section 6.2 mandates day-level random intercepts; section 6.3 gives starting values for alpha, theta, sigma, nu and beta and stops, so the day effect's scale has no pre-committed prior. HalfNormal(0.5) is in use, matching alpha's scale, and carries its provenance in code | A ruling, written into framework_v1.yaml | **Before the hash lock.** Under section 0 an arbitrary value gets one revision window, on synthetic corpora, before any partner's data |
| TD-019 | A condition almost determined by the day defeats M1 | Measured 10 Oct 2026: with prevalence swinging 2 to 95 per cent across days there is no within-day contrast left, partial pooling leaves part of the day effect in the residual, and theta absorbs it - theta -0.45 with posterior sd 0.14 against a true theta of zero, with zero divergences, r_hat inside 1.01 and bulk ess far above 400. Non-identifiability, not a sampling failure, so the framework's diagnostics cannot see it. Affects S10 (Wednesday EIA), B12 (the day is the outcome), S1 (session), S6 (overnight carry) | A ruling: either a pre-registered identifiability check per test, or a different model for day-linked conditions | **Before the hash lock**, because it changes what some of the eighteen tests may claim |

| TD-020 | M1's `alpha` and `tau_day` fail diagnostics in two regimes, and the low-noise one is a parameterisation choice | Measured 10 Oct 2026. With a true `tau_day` of 0.055 the scale sits against its boundary at zero, the non-centred `z_day` terms become unidentified, and `tau_day`'s r_hat was 1.017 at 2,000 draws against the framework's 1.01 limit; 4,000 draws gave 1.003. theta mixed well throughout (ess 558 to 2913, r_hat <= 1.0012), so the difficulty is confined to the nuisance scale - but poor mixing there means the day intercepts are under-explored, which can distort theta's interval, and the interval is what the promotion criterion reads. `HalfNormal(0.5)` puts about 16 per cent of prior draws below 0.1, so a real share of SBC replications land in this region | Diagnosed 10 Oct 2026 by logging every SBC replication's true parameters: `alpha` fails at **low sigma** (median 0.44 against 1.63 clean), where each day's intercept is sharply identified and the non-centred form is the wrong parameterisation - the known result of Papaspiliopoulos, Roberts and Skold 2007. Divergences sit at **high sigma** (2.27) with a drowned day effect. nu does not differ between groups, which is why flooring it changed nothing. Next: the centred form, pre-registered - low-sigma failures should largely vanish | **Settled by measurement, 10 Oct 2026, and parked as a ruling for the kickoff.** The centred form was tried over the same 300 replications: 23 per cent diverged, tau_day completion 30 per cent, theta 73 - far worse, because under these priors most mass sits where day effects are drowned in noise, which is non-centred territory; centred works only where tau_day is large relative to sigma (clean median tau_day 0.61, sigma 0.94). **Non-centred is the parameterisation.** theta is calibrated under it on three runs. Its shortfall is alpha and tau_day in the low-sigma corner, about 13 per cent of the prior, and sigma near 0.44R is an implausibly tight regime for discretionary retail - a judgment the first partner histories will test. The kickoff ruling: SBC blocks on reported parameters and reports nuisance ones with their completion, because theta's own calibration is the test of whether the nuisance structure does harm. Hybrid parameterisation parked as TD-022 |

| TD-021 | Floor `nu` at 2 on correctness grounds | Real returns have fat tails and finite variance; Gamma(2, 0.1) admits infinite variance. Proposed as a fix for 59 diverged replications, its prediction failed (4.3 per cent against 5.9), so it is withdrawn as a fix and stands only as its own argument. Cost per replication halved with the floor, so the tail makes fits slow, not failed | A ruling on the argument alone | The kickoff. Must not ride in under a fix that fixed nothing |

| TD-022 | Per-day hybrid parameterisation for M1 | Non-centred fails alpha and tau_day on about 13 per cent of the prior (low sigma); centred fails on about 70 per cent (everything else). A per-day switch by data density (Betancourt and Girolami 2015) could recover part of the 13 per cent, for two parameters no trader sees, at real complexity | Evidence from real partner histories that sigma near 0.44R occurs | Not before MVP. Revisit if partner data lands in the low-sigma corner |
