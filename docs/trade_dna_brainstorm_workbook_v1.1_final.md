## 0. How to use this workbook

- **This is not the thesis.** The thesis decides; this workbook explores. It may hold competing ideas, unresolved tensions, strong opinions, dead ideas and wild options. Its job is to make the thesis better, not to pretend everything inside is true.
- **Status vocabulary (shared with the thesis):** **FACT** · **DECIDED** · **HYPOTHESIS** · **UNKNOWN** · **EXPERIMENT** · **PARKED** · **REJECTED**. Ideas here also carry **CANDIDATE** (promising, not decided) and **WILD** (speculative, kept on purpose).
- **Promotion gate.** An idea moves BRAINSTORM → HYPOTHESIS → DECIDED → SPECIFICATION only by: (1) writing it as a falsifiable statement; (2) defining what evidence would support it and what would kill it; (3) running the cheapest test that meaningfully changes belief; (4) recording the result in the decision log; (5) only then promoting it into the thesis. **Never silently turn an assumption into architecture.**
- **Operating rule.** A good brainstorm gets narrower over time. It ends by removing ideas that do not survive evidence, not by adding more.
- **Source tags.** [C] the founding idea · [A] [B] [C-n] [D-n] the four reviews · [GPT] the ChatGPT thesis and workbook · [DS] the DeepSeek brainstorm and thesis · [+] ours.

### The Canon — the thinking floor (Thesis v1.4 §0 inherits it; see freeze corrections 3 and 4 for what the thesis has since amended)
1. Trade DNA is PlusEV's doormat: a free, one-time, evidence-backed diagnosis of how a trader trades — the front door to the PlusEV platform, deliberately not the final product.
2. The input is trade-level data — every trade, fills where available — never a P&L number, reconstructed into the Canonical Trade Record.
3. The atomic unit is the Finding: a claim with evidence, sample size, effect size, confidence tier and a lifecycle. Nothing reaches a trader that is not a promoted Finding.
4. The Evidence Standard governs everything: Fact → Pattern → Hypothesis → Action → Validation; tiers Established / Likely / Exploratory; adversarial validation; false-discovery control; hold-out within the trader's own history; "Not enough evidence yet" is a valid result.
5. The Trader Model has four DNAs — Strategy, Risk, Execution, Behaviour — plus Process Health, summarised as a Process Score that sits on top of the diagnosis and never replaces it. Market/Regime DNA is the fifth dimension, later. Archetypes are emergent, probabilistic summaries, never the lens.
6. The signature number is Avoidable Loss — the cost of behaviour the trader could have avoided, stated conservatively in his currency. The Process Score is our candidate for the category's standard number; the market decides whether it becomes one.
7. The deterministic research engine is the source of truth for every number. AI is the research interface and orchestrator — import mapping, narration, explanation, orchestration, and hypothesis proposals to humans — and can never create or alter a Finding.
8. The engine computes every relevant prescription and ranks them as Next Best Move; the show reveals them as the trader's interest shows. **One active primary experiment at a time**, replayed on his own history first, with length and success criteria set by the measurement design before it starts, and never raising his risk.
9. North Star: Trader Improvement — the change in risk-adjusted expectancy per trade over the experiment window versus the pre-intervention baseline, regime-adjusted, with process-quality guardrails. Engagement depth is a leading indicator only.
10. The show is the product: a six-screen doormat arc, one cognitive job per screen, interactive and animated evidence, a DNA Map, hooked from the first screen, basic to depth, built for short attention spans — and insight-dense on every screen.
11. Every action is a signal. Everything that must survive the Evidence Standard is computed before the show; what to show next is computed while he reads, from his activity and his responses. The first aha is never behind an ask.
12. Value exchange is reciprocity: every ask has a product reason the trader can understand; no ask that only benefits PlusEV; no phone number until an alert product exists.
13. Growth is built in: the DNA Card, the monthly DNA, educators and prop firms, and public research from consented aggregates.
14. Trust is architecture: an entity-level wall between Trade DNA data and the fund; least privilege; transparent deletion; read-only connections; counsel before launch; every Finding traceable to evidence, method and version.
15. Sequence by uncertainty: Phase 0 Discover (concierge, 20–50 design partners) → Phase 1 Doormat → Phase 2 Loop → Phase 3 Platform → Phase 4 Scale. Launch target: India first, global-ready — pending the CEO's decision before Phase 0. Product name: pending the CEO's decision at the Phase 0 review, after the trademark search.

## 1. The questions we brainstorm around

Every idea below serves one of these. If it answers none, it is decoration.

1. Which three outputs would make a trader say, *"PlusEV found something I could never have found myself"*?
2. What can we reliably know from 100–500 trades — and how do we say the rest honestly?
3. How does a trader *know* we were right, without taking our word for it?
4. What is genuinely valuable to a trader who is losing and has no demonstrated edge?
5. What makes a trader finish all six screens — and share the last one?
6. What would make a trader come back every week without being nagged?
7. What would a prop firm, a broker or an educator pay for?
8. How do we prove PlusEV *caused* an improvement, not the market?
9. What makes the brand trusted when a hedge fund owns the product?
10. What is the smallest thing we can ship that proves the brain behind the show is extraordinary?
11. What must PlusEV understand about traders — and do with that understanding — that a journal, a broker or a generic AI cannot?
12. What would we regret building in year one?

## 10. Open research questions

**Statistical and machine learning**
- Minimum sample for a reliable probabilistic archetype (hypothesis: 150+ trades)?
- How to infer a risk unit for traders without stops — position size × volatility, adverse excursion, or both?
- Avoidable Loss: the right conservative bound, and how to attribute loss to overlapping leak conditions without double-counting.
- Process Score calibration: weights, the confidence band, stability across framework versions, resistance to gaming.
- How to detect genuine improvement versus luck (hypothesis: Bayesian change-point on process metrics, regime-adjusted)?
- Matched comparison groups from the population without leaking individual data.
- Interest-signal modelling: which signals predict acceptance, adoption and sharing — without turning into engagement-bait?
- Non-stationarity in a trader's behaviour; regime shifts in an individual's edge.
- Optimal intervention sequencing; bandits for prescription selection once outcomes accumulate.
- Transfer across instruments and markets; survivorship bias; detecting a trader gaming the diagnosis.
- Differential privacy budgets for published aggregates; automated faithfulness testing of narration.

**Behavioural**
- What share of traders change behaviour after a diagnosis? Which Finding changes it most — cost, mechanism or move?
- Does strength-before-leak framing improve adoption? Does the "Wrong" button build or erode trust?
- Which screen loses people, and why? Do DNA Cards get shared — by whom, why?
- Does the monthly DNA improve retention? Does experiment completion predict subscription?

**Product**
- Six-screen completion, evidence-acceptance, experiment-completion and free-to-paid targets; the subscription retention curve.
- Which three outputs win Phase 0? Which animation actually carries evidence?
